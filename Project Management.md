
Project management is the coordination of your processes, tools, team
members, and skills so you can deliver projects that exceed your goals.
A *project* is a set of well-defined activities that:
- have a start and an end
- realize a goal
- made by a team of people
- using a set of resources
- a unique process, not a repetitive business process in a company
### Project vs Operations

|                 | Operations                                                      | Projects                                                                                             |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goal            | implicit                                                        | Project scope                                                                                        |
| Quality         | Quality standard for a product                                  | Project quality goals                                                                                |
| Time            | Quality standard for a product                                  | Limited duration, window of opportunity                                                              |
| Cost            | Operation costs                                                 | budget                                                                                               |
| Characteristics | Business Processes in company                                   | Limited duration                                                                                     |
| Examples        | Suppliers payment, shipping orders,<br>factory production lines | R&D, engineering constructions,<br>marketing campaigns, move house,<br>wedding, new product creation |
