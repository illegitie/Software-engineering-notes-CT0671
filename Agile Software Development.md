## Agile Methods
These methods are geared to rapid soft. del.
- Soft is developed and delivered in increments and process docs and bureaucracy are minimized
- Focus of devs is on the code, rather than on docs
Agile helps to avoid soft failure and reduce overheads in the soft process.

| Principle            | Description                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Customer involvement | Customers should be involved in dev process. Their role is to provide and prioritize new sys requirements and to evaluate the iterations of the system |
| Incremental Delivery | Soft is developed in increments with the customer requirements included in each increment                                                              |
| People not process   | Skills of devs should be recognized and exploited. Team should be left to develop their own ways of working without perspective processes              |
| Embrace change       | Expect sys reqs to change and so design the sys to accommodate these changes                                                                           |
| Maintain simplicity  | Focus on simplicity in both the soft being developed and in the dev process. Actively work to eliminate complexity when possible.                      |

## Stories
In XP, user reqs are expressed as user stories or scenarios.
These are written on cards and the development team break them down into
implementation tasks. These tasks are the basis of schedule and cost estimates.

- A **technique** to informally define what the system should do
	A persona identifies and describes typical users
	A persona is a subset of an actor of a use-case
	- Ex persona1: female, middle age, professional, high income, married with children
	- Ex persona2: male, young, student, low income, not married
	For each persona describe life scenarios
	- Persona1, work day: wake up, breakfast, drive children to school, drive to office ...
	- Persona1, week end day: ...
	For each persona /scenario identify possible interaction with application/object
