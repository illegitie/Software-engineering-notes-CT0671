#req-engineering
The hardest part of building a software system is deciding what to build
A process of identifying, analysing, validating, deciding, structuring requirements for the system to be:
- that the customer requires from a sys
- and the constraints under which it operates and developed
# framework for RE process
![[req_analysis.png]]
1. Understanding the problem: elicitation
2. Describing the problem: specification
3. Agreeing on the nature of the problem: validation
4. Agreeing on the limits of the problem: negotiation
5. This is an iterative process
Natural language specifications are dangerous due to ambiguity, implicit assumptions, and varying interpretations.
