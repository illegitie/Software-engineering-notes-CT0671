
### Requirement engineering -> Architecture design -> Implementation

### Each activity depends on the previous one
- to design one must know the requierements
- to implement, one must know the design and the requierements
### Software engineering is about management of the resources
#### recources are:
- people
- time
- money
#### Management activites:
- Project Management
	- Assign work and monitor progress
	- Estimate and control budget
- Configuration Management
	- Identify, store documents and units
	- Keep track of relationships and history
- Quality Assurance
	- Define quality goals
	- Define how work will be done
	- Control results
