#uml
# **Unified Modelling Language**
System modeling is the process of developing abstract models of a system, with each model presenting a different view or perspective.
- **Purpose:** Helps analysts understand system functionality and serves as a communication tool with customers.
- **UML Origins:** Adopted as a standard in 1997 (version 1.1) based on the work of Booch, Jacobson, and Rumbaugh. Currently developed by the *Object Management Group* (OMG).
# **UML Diagram Types**
UML includes several diagram types to represent structural (static) and dynamic aspects of a system.
- **Structural:** Class, Component, Deployment.
- **Dynamic:** Use Case, Sequence, State, Activity, Collaboration.
# **[[UML Structural Models & Class Diagrams]]**
Structural models display the organization of a system in terms of its components and their relationships.
# **[[UML Case Diagrams]]**
Use Case diagrams provide a high-level view of what a system does and identify the external users interacting with it. They are usually defined _before_ class diagrams to establish user requirements.
